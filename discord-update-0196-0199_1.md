Dev Update | Patches 0196–0199

**New**
- Added a new game to Grimsby's cart: Dice — pick a number, wager your gold, and roll. Land it exactly for triple your gold back, a face either side gets you half back, anything else is a bust
- Added a Stakes selector (1x–5x) to Grimsby's card game and High Roller — raises the fee for a proportionally bigger reward
- Added a Roll Again button once a card's revealed — straight back to a fresh set of cards without closing the window
- Added three new stats: Gold spent at Grimsby, Grimsby jackpots, and Grimsby busts

**Stash & Inventory**
- Added a Stash limit — starts at 10 slots, buyable up in the Guild Hall with the new Stash Expansion upgrade (+5 per level, up to 8 levels). Loot from quests, raids, and Grimsby always still comes in — only voluntary purchases/buybacks are blocked once full, with a "stash is full" notice
- Added a gear-type filter to the Stash so you can sort it down to just Weapons, Helmets, and so on
- Costs on the Enhance, Craft/Enchant, and vendor buttons now show a gold icon next to the number

**Fixes**
- Fixed: setting a hero's title to "None" wasn't actually clearing it — it silently kept showing their last-earned title instead
- Fixed: heroes out on a raid showed up on the desktop companion as idle and available instead of looking busy
- Changed: the desktop companion's "+N more" line now says how many heroes are idle vs. questing (and injured, if any)
- Fixed: the fullscreen button in Guild Hall could get stuck — toggling to fullscreen and clicking again sometimes wouldn't bring back windowed mode
- Fixed: the desktop companion's left/right hero-cycling arrows could sit at different heights per hero and occasionally miss clicks
- Fixed: Gold Storage's cap wasn't being enforced everywhere gold comes in — selling gear, curios, or materials could push you past your storage limit
- Fixed three Guild Hall upgrade cards (Chain Tactics, Board Runner, Board Warden) that were missing their descriptions

**Changes**
- Added a minimise button to the Guild Hall window, and renamed "Back to desktop" to "Close"
