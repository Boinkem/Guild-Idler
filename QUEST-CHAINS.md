# Guild Idler — Quest Chain Outline

18 chains, levels 2–55. Organized in the order a player actually encounters
them. Three throughlines exist (marked below); everything else is
intentionally standalone — same world, same tone, no forced connection.

**Threads:**
- 🏰 **The Keep** — a five-chain arc adapted from a real tabletop campaign: winning a keep, defending it, earning allies.
- 🩸 **The Harrower** — this world's own orchestrating villain (demons as harvesters, undead as the harvest). Never fully named or shown. Three chains plant it; one distant chain pays it off.
- 🎭 Standalone — same tone and world, no connection required or implied.

---

## Level 2 — The Miller's Problem
*Starter chain. Standalone.*

The guild's first real job, deliberately mundane at the start: rats in a
cellar. Escalates across three short stages into something that's been
down there longer than the mill has, and is patient in a way rats aren't.
Ends with the smallest, earliest, most deniable hint of the World-Ender at
the very bottom of the game — a detail meant to mean nothing on a first
read.

**Title:** First Real Job

---

## Level 6 — The Goblin King's Warband 🎭
*Standalone.*

Three goblin warbands, one contested crown. The crown turns out to belong
to nobody in this story — it was stolen from somewhere else entirely, a
loose thread deliberately left untied.

**Title:** Warband-Breaker

---

## Level 8 — The Crow's Warning 🏰 *(Keep, part 1)*
*Standalone opening, but the first domino in the Keep arc.*

Demons attack Thornhollow with method, not randomness — searching, not
razing. A crow that won't leave leads the trail to Greywick, where an
alchemist needs spider venom to test a cure against whatever's spreading.
Ends with the finished vials on the road to IronRest Bastion, setting up
the next chain's opening.

**Title:** Crow-Friend

---

## Level 10 — Search for the Ancient Crown 🎭
*Standalone.*

Three fragments of a crown, three very different hiding places. Nobody
involved ever explains why it was worth hiding in the first place — the
kind of mystery that's the point, not a gap.

**Title:** Crownbearer

---

## Level 11 — The Harrower's Foot 🩸 *(Harrower, part 1)*
*Standalone on its own, first thread of the Harrower arc.*

A second village culled exactly like an earlier one — searched, not
raided. The demons responsible fight like they're being watched by
something that will ask what they found. No name yet, just a pattern.

**Title:** Ash-Reader

---

## Level 14 — Lost Kingdom Expedition 🎭
*Standalone.*

A sealed vault gives up what it was built to protect. The kingdom that
built it stays exactly as lost as before — some things aren't meant to be
found twice.

**Title:** Kingdom-Finder

---

## Level 15 — A Demon General's Ledger 🩸 *(Harrower, part 2)*

Escalation from Harrower's Foot: a disciplined demon column, led by
something that gives orders instead of following hunger. Its defeat
surfaces a literal ledger of tallied victims — and, in the margins, the
first and only place in the entire game the Harrower is named.

**Title:** Tally-Breaker

---

## Level 16 — Proving the Bastion 🏰 *(Keep, part 2 — the Keep is won)*

Captain Maeryn Thorne puts the guild to work: contracts, then beast hunts
that don't behave like ordinary beasts, culminating in an assault on a
Demon Lord's fort. Winning it earns the guild an actual home — the Keep —
in exchange for a standing partnership with IronRest. Everything from here
on that mentions "the Keep" as an existing place is set *after* this chain.

**Title:** Lord of the Keep

---

## Level 18 — The Dragon Hunt 🎭
*Standalone.*

Emberfell, northernmost of the holdfasts, is burning. A straightforward,
deliberately old-fashioned dragon hunt — no deeper scheme, no connection to
anything else. Some threats are just dragons.

**Title:** Dragonbane

---

## Level 19 — What the Culled Become 🩸 *(Harrower, part 3 — plants the Hollow King seed)*

The payoff of the Harrower's name from level 15: villages it's culled
report graves opening from the inside. The taken don't stay taken — they
come back changed, still counted. The investigation ends at an old
barrow-ground that has started, very quietly, to stir. Nobody connects this
to anything yet. The Hollow King's chain, 26 levels later, is the payoff.

**Title:** Grave-Watcher

---

## Level 20 — Granite Crossing 🏰 *(Keep side story)*

The first thing on the Keep's own war table: Granite Crossing, home of the
Stone Masons Guild, gone quiet for weeks. Raiders under a man named Garrick
are holding it. His defeat earns the guild the masons' help rebuilding the
Keep itself.

**Title:** Garrick's End

---

## Level 22 — Demon Fortress Assault 🎭 *(quietly sets up Hollow Choir)*

An iron gate finally opens for someone not trying to escape. One stage,
"Silence the Warding Choir," plants a detail on purpose: the singing
*stops*, but the chain's own text hints this isn't really over — "you tell
yourself that, going in." Level 26 confirms it wasn't.

**Title:** Hellgate Breaker

---

## Level 24 — The Farm at the Edge 🏰 *(Keep side story)*

A border farm goes silent. Raiders under Captain Vayne are dug in — and
she's mid-ritual when found, something poured into her paid for with more
than she has left to give. The clearest echo of the Harrower's whole
transactional scheme outside the Harrower's own chains, without ever naming
it directly.

**Title:** Farmwarden

---

## Level 26 — The Hollow Choir 🎭 *(pays off Demon Fortress)*

The choir from level 22 didn't stop — it relocated. Nine years of
unbroken singing in an old cathedral, explicitly called back to "a choir
like this once before, at the fortress." The one deliberate exception to
"nothing connects" in this list: two chains, sixteen levels apart, about
the same unfinished thing.

**Title:** Choir-Silencer

---

## Level 32 — The Pale Rider 🏰 *(Keep arc capstone)*

A squire from the Paladins of the Ashen Hand arrives at the Keep asking for
help few would agree to give. The hunt tracks "the Pale Rider" to Saint
Aurlias Rest, where graves are emptying toward the church rather than away
from it — something on the other side is calling them home. He's waiting
behind those doors. Two artifact-tier rewards granted together: Sword of
the Ashen Hand and Bulwark of the War Saint.

**Title:** Ashen-Sworn

---

## Level 34 — The World-Ender's Vigil 🎭
*Standalone, cosmic register, deliberately never explained.*

Nameless, older than the first kingdom, watched over by "Watchers in the
Dark Between Stars." No orchestrator, no scheme, no name given because it's
never needed one. Stays completely disconnected from the Harrower on
purpose — some things in this world are older than anything with a plan.

**Title:** World's End

---

## Level 45 — The Hollow King's Return 🩸 *(pays off What the Culled Become)*

The barrow-ground that stirred quietly since level 19 has stopped being
quiet. Explicitly *not* the Harrower's servant — woken as an unintended
casualty of its rising necromantic tide, a second power the Harrower
doesn't control and may not have wanted awake either.

**Title:** Kingslayer Twice Over

---

## Level 55 — Requiem for the Last God 🎭
*Capstone. Standalone, but remembers the guild's own history.*

The deepest expedition the guild has ever chartered, no patron, nobody
left to pay for it. Explicitly acknowledges the guild has "stared down a
nameless thing at the edge of the world and a king who would not stay
buried" — a sense of accumulated legend referencing World-Ender and Hollow
King by description, not by forcing them into one shared plot.

**Title:** Requiem-Bearer
